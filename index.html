Common Questions

What areas do you serve?

We're based in Lemoore and serve communities across California's Central Valley — Fresno, Clovis, Madera, Sanger, Selma, Reedley, Kingsburg, Hanford, Visalia, and Tulare. Not sure if you're in range? Just call and ask.

Are you licensed, insured, and bonded?

Yes. Avila Infrastructure & Contracting is fully licensed, insured, and bonded in the State of California — so your project and your property are protected.

What kinds of work do you handle?

Utility infrastructure (power, communications, and underground systems), agricultural solutions (service poles, pump power, and utility installation), and utility installation (overhead and underground lines and service connections).

How do I get an estimate?

Fill out the form below or call (559) 349-4392 with a few details about your project, and Jonathan will get back to you within 24 hours.

Who actually does the work?

Avila is owner-operated. Jonathan Avila oversees every project personally — no subcontractors and no runaround, so you're dealing directly with the person accountable for the job.

Can you schedule around the growing season?

Absolutely. The Central Valley growing season doesn't wait, so we schedule around harvests, plantings, and weather windows to keep your operation running.

Do you handle emergency or urgent work?

We offer emergency and urgent-work availability, though it can vary with current workload. For anything time-sensitive, call (559) 349-4392 directly for the fastest response.
